# maji-market-scan: Competitor and Market Scan

Check the market before money or weeks go into building or pricing. Final output: a dated one-page scan (buyer, alternatives, price table, positioning map, gap, first-ten path, verdict) that hands off to maji-offer and maji-outreach.

## Step 0: your context

Read `me/profile.md` if it exists (Identity, Brand, Money and tax, Folders, Voice and language). If `me/overrides/maji-market-scan.md` exists, follow it wherever it differs from this file. If something this skill needs is missing, ask once, use the answer, and offer to save it to the profile.

## When to use / when not

- Use before starting a new product, service package, course or marketplace listing.
- Use before pricing, when the owner has no outside reference point.
- Use when a client asks "is anyone else doing this?" and needs an honest answer.
- Not a full industry report or market-sizing study; one page, a few hours at most.
- Not for researching private individuals; companies, products and public listings only.

## Steps

1. **Frame the scan.** One line each: what is offered, who pays, the job they hire it for, region and language, and the decision this scan supports (build or not, price band, positioning).
2. **List the alternatives.** 5 to 8 entries across four kinds: direct competitors, indirect substitutes (spreadsheet, agency, freelancer, generic SaaS), local players, and doing nothing.
3. **Capture each one.** Table columns: name, kind, target buyer, core promise in their words, price and unit (per month, per head, per project), free tier, notable strength, notable weakness from reviews, source link, date checked.
4. **Date and label everything.** Every price carries the date checked and whether it was listed, quoted on request, or inferred.
5. **Price benchmark.** Summarise the range in the buyer's unit: low, typical, premium, with which alternative sits where.
6. **Positioning map.** Pick two axes the buyer actually cares about (for example price vs effort to adopt, generic vs sector-specific, self-serve vs done-for-you).
7. **Name the gap.** One or two sentences: which buyer is underserved and why the offer wins there.
8. **First-ten path.** Where the first ten paying customers come from: warm network and referrals first, then one community where the buyers gather, then targeted direct outreach to a short list of companies.
9. **Verdict.** One of: build, build narrower, price at band X, or stop.

## Composes with

- **maji-offer**: takes the price band and positioning into the quote.
- **maji-outreach**: takes the first-ten path and segments into a sequence.
- **maji-proposal**: uses the scan as background and need evidence.
- **maji-propose**: when the verdict changes the build plan.
- **maji-mode**: one recommendation, no three-option dumps.
